About

I'm a Senior Lighting TD and Unreal Technical Artist with 23 years of experience across feature animation, live-action VFX, and episodic television, at studios including LAIKA, DreamWorks, Double Negative, Digital Domain, and Zoic. Dual citizen of the USA and UK.

I'm currently expanding into generative VFX and technical art — combining a production background in lighting, Unreal Engine, and Houdini with emerging GenAI tools to develop experimental, artist-driven workflows for visual storytelling. Outside of production work, I shoot photography and build small graphics and pipeline projects.
